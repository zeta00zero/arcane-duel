Arcane Duel Chronicles - Audio credits
======================================

Music and sound effects
-----------------------
All music and sound effects in this game are ORIGINAL and PROCEDURAL.
They are composed and synthesised in real time by JavaScript code using the
browser's Web Audio API (oscillators, filtered noise and envelopes), written
specifically for Arcane Duel Chronicles.

- No audio files, samples or recordings are included or downloaded.
- No third-party music or sound libraries are used.
- No music from Yu-Gi-Oh!, any anime or any other copyrighted work is used,
  quoted or imitated.

Source: i_audio.js section of index.html (embedded in the game file)
Author: Arcane Duel Chronicles project (generated for the game owner)
Licence: owned by the game owner, together with the rest of the game code.
No third-party attribution is required.

Tracks (all procedural, original compositions, seamless loops; v9c/v9d epic guitar-rock revamp)
All nine themes share one synthesized rock rig: double-tracked distorted rhythm guitars (WaveShaper amp +
cab EQ with cached curves), lead guitar with slides, bends and vibrato, clean/crunch arpeggio guitar,
rock bass, rock kit, strings, choir and brass. Every melody, riff and progression is original.
  Chronicles Overture  - 150 BPM, E (v9c epic rock rewrite). Synthesized distorted guitars (double-tracked
                         power chords and palm-muted chugs, WaveShaper amp + cab EQ), lead guitar with slides,
                         bends and vibrato, twin-lead final chorus, driving bass, rock kit with tom fills,
                         strings/choir build-ups. Menu, character select, deck builder.
  Duel Standby         - 140 BPM, F# minor (v9d). Driving battle rock: syncopated riff, crunchy verse lead over
                         clean arpeggios, build, big chorus, half-time breakdown, guitar solo ending in twin harmony.
                         Default duel theme (Athletics Stadium and any unmapped arena).
  Banner of the Dawn   - 108 BPM, Bb major (v9d). Heroic power-metal march: brass fanfare and timpani intro,
                         twin-lead march, choir + brass chorus, triumphant fanfare section.
                         Kaito's castle, Toma's forest, Shaim's rift peaks, Aoi Yuki's concert stage.
  Ember Lair           - 168 BPM, A Phrygian dominant (v9d). Dragon thrash metal: low-tuned gallop riffs,
                         double-kick chorus with choir, shred solo, twin-lead final chorus. Ryuga's dragon lair.
  Neon Circuit         - 126 BPM, C Dorian (v9d). Synthwave cyber-rock: pulse-synth melody and pluck arps over
                         djent-style chugs, guitar + supersaw chorus hook, breakdown, synth/guitar trade-off.
                         Mira's machine world, Ben's robot hangar, Weisheng's racetrack, Clement's hobby con, Jason's pawnshop alley.
  Doki Doki Toybox     - 168 BPM, F major (v9d). Cute pop-punk toy box: bright light-crunch guitars, music box
                         and glockenspiel, marimba/flute bridge over clean arpeggios, twin-lead final chorus.
                         Lily's fairy lake, Wei Yao's idol stage, Sabrina's toy town, Keith's cat cafe,
                         Wei Yao - The 2nd Coming's derby dome, Kimberly's cosplay stage.
  Moonlit Arcana       - 96 BPM, D minor in 6/8 (v9d). Mystic symphonic rock: harp and choir intro, flute omen,
                         6/8 rock ride, choir climax with twin lead in harmonic minor, violin moonfall.
                         Sakura's magician room, Si Xing's silent theatre.
  Blood Moon Rite      - 72 BPM, B minor (v9d). Slow, sad, dramatic guitar ballad: clean arpeggiated guitar and
                         piano lament, crying lead with long bends and wide vibrato, strings/choir/timpani rise,
                         heavy slow power-chord climax with twin lead, piano and clean-guitar ashes. Not upbeat.
                         Eryll's Nemesis Coliseum, Dark Kelvin's gloom cathedral.
  Kopi Break           - 92 BPM, G major (swing, v9d). Laid-back guitar groove: clean chorus-guitar comping,
                         upright bass, electric piano, warm low-gain lead, vibraphone cafe section with guitar fills.
                         Christopher's kopitiam, Lemon's cubicle hell, Hisyam's fried chicken night market, Eunice's cosy bakery.
Jingles: ace-entrance stinger, victory, defeat, duel start, coin toss (v9d: synthesized guitar flourishes
added to ace, victory, defeat and duel start; lengths unchanged; limited so they never clip).
Style references named by the player were used for genre, mood, tempo and instrumentation only; no melody,
chord sequence or riff from any existing work was copied or approximated.

Video clips
-----------
The summon/victory video clips (assets/video) carry their own audio, generated
for this project. Game music is lowered while a clip plays.

Online multiplayer
==================
Online duels use PeerJS 1.5.5 (https://peerjs.com, https://github.com/peers/peerjs),
bundled (minified, unmodified) inside index.html. Licence: MIT.
PeerJS's bundle also contains these libraries:
  - webrtc-adapter 9.0.6 (BSD-3-Clause)
  - eventemitter3 4.0.7 (MIT)
  - peerjs-js-binarypack 2.1.0 (MIT)
  - sdp 3.2.2 (MIT)
Their full licence texts follow below.

Online services used at run time (free, no account):
  - PeerJS public cloud signalling server 0.peerjs.com (run by the PeerJS project).
    It only introduces the two browsers to each other. Game data then flows
    directly between the players over an encrypted WebRTC data channel.
  - Public STUN servers: stun.l.google.com, stun1.l.google.com, stun.cloudflare.com.
  - PeerJS's public best-effort TURN relay (eu-0/us-0.turn.peerjs.com), used only
    when a direct connection is impossible.
